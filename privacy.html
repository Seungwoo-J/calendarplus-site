Calendar+ privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Calendar+ is an Obsidian plugin. Google account integration is optional and requires your authorization through Google.

Google data accessed

  • Account email, to identify connected accounts in the plugin.
  • Google Calendar information and events, for read-only display in Calendar+.
  • Google Tasks lists and tasks, including titles, completion state, notes and hierarchy, to synchronize the lists you select with a local checklist. This requires permission to create, update, organize and delete tasks.

How data is used and stored

The plugin communicates directly with Google’s authorization and API services to connect your account and synchronize data. Google Calendar events are cached in the plugin’s local settings. Google Tasks information and synchronization identifiers are stored in a Markdown checklist in your vault and in plugin synchronization metadata. This information may be available to other plugins or services that can access or synchronize your vault.

On supported Windows installations, Calendar+ stores refresh tokens and client secrets in Obsidian SecretStorage protected using Windows user-bound encryption. Access tokens are kept in memory. Other software running under the same Windows user may still be able to access those credentials.

Calendar+ has an optional local read-only integration for external tools; if you enable it, authorized local clients can access the context it exposes. Vault synchronization, backups, other plugins and other tools you choose to use are subject to their own handling practices.

Sharing and control

The Google integration sends necessary information to Google to authenticate and synchronize. Calendar+ does not require a Calendar+ developer-operated server for Google synchronization. The plugin does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

You can disconnect an account in Calendar+ and revoke its access from your Google account’s third-party connections. Removing an account in Calendar+ does not itself revoke Google permissions or delete Google tasks and local checklist content. You can manage or delete local checklist and cached data in your vault, and manage Google data in your Google account.

Calendar+’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
