Calendar+ for Obsidian

Calendar+ is an Obsidian plugin for viewing calendars and managing tasks in your vault.

Google integration

If you choose to connect a Google account, Calendar+ reads Google Calendar events for display and synchronizes selected Google Tasks lists with a local Markdown checklist. Task changes made in either place can be synchronized in both directions. Google Calendar access is read-only.

Google account authorization is optional and is managed through Google’s consent screen. The plugin runs on your device and communicates directly with Google APIs for this integration.

Privacy policy and Google data use

For support, contact the email listed on the Google authorization screen for your OAuth client.
